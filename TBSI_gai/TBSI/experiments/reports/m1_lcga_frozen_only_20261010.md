This is a new training-contract experiment, not a retuning of the previous joint-continuation run. Only `search_refiner.*` may update. Paired per-sequence AUC: 14 gains, 16 losses, median difference -0.0173, mean difference -0.2984, bootstrap 95% interval [-0.9906, +0.2629]. Removing the largest-gain sequence changes mean delta to -0.3874. Sequence median center error improved in 10/30, worsened in 10/30 and tied in 10/30; the median sequence difference is 0 px. Across 22,770 paired valid frames, the pooled median center error is 3.3541 px in both arms. The largest AUC losses are `openningumbrella` -6.10 and `bikeboystrong` -5.20; the largest gain is `whiteboy\`head` +2.28. These are descriptive error cases, not a basis for retuning on this dev split.

The learned outer gates are RGB -0.008324 and TIR +0.008706. The four internal curvature alphas are [-0.001388, +0.003559, +0.002427, +0.000381]. A same-checkpoint intervention on three **previously used**, train-side diagnostic sequences found trained-minus-curvature-zero AUC differences of 0.00 (`2ndcarcome`), +1.48 (`3whitemen`) and -0.27 (`4runeight`). The latter two can show large closed-loop box differences even though real-batch curvature gradients began very small. This establishes a mixed immediate trajectory effect on those three sequences only; it is not a matched retraining control and does not identify a general curvature benefit.

**Decision: stop this LCGA-only training contract before full training/test.** The predeclared center-localization criterion (at least 20/30 improvements and a negative median sequence center-error delta) fails decisively, AUC is slightly negative with uncertainty across zero, and inference is about 20% slower. Relative to the earlier joint-continuation failure, freezing M1 removes the head/fusion co-adaptation concern but does not reveal a broad localization gain. This does **not** prove that all local spatial modeling is ineffective, nor that CENTER-head adaptation is the true bottleneck; a separate matched head-only or search-to-head readout experiment would be needed for that claim. No full-244 test was run and the protected M1 test metric must not be compared numerically with this train-side dev30.
