# M1 AMCM E2 10-epoch 50-sequence validation (2026-10-17)

## Protocol

E2 supervised post-fusion AMCM was kept unchanged. The run initialized from protected M1 epoch15, trained only `amcm_reliability.*` for 10 epochs with 512 samples/epoch, batch 2, FP32, and one DataLoader worker. The fixed test manifest contains 50 LasHeR testingset sequences after removing the three known invalid sequences; its SHA256 is `a76af65939e9940aa491cb5cd1e2a645cfcb7c2d9ca0a99309656c894940e795`.

Training and evaluation used independent config `M1_AMCM_E2_10EP_50SEQ_20261017`, seed 42, and the epoch10 checkpoint. Evaluation used the same FP32 tracker and search/template parameters. The first 18 sequences were completed serially; remaining sequences used two workers after verifying identical checkpoint and tracker settings. Existing outputs were skipped by filename.

## Result

| Scope | AUC | OP50 | OP75 | Precision | Norm Precision |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| E2 10 epoch, fixed 50 sequences | 54.72 | 67.73 | 45.58 | 69.22 | 65.17 |
| E2 5 epoch, first 30 sequences | 49.50 | 61.71 | 41.83 | 63.92 | 60.07 |

The 10-epoch/50-sequence result is higher than the earlier 5-epoch/30-sequence proxy, but the scopes and schedules differ, so this is not a paired improvement claim. It remains below the protected M1 full-test reference and does not establish promotion of AMCM.

## Status

`EXPANDED-50SEQ-COMPLETE / NOT-SUPPORTED-FOR-PROMOTION`. The run confirms that the training and evaluation path is reproducible over 50 sequences, but the supervised reliability branch does not yet provide evidence for a robust tracking gain. Further work should use an out-of-fold candidate-utility target or a matched M1 50-sequence control before changing architecture or unfreezing fusion/head.
