# M1-fixed CENTER strip-score short screening (2026-10-10)

## Preregistered contract

Question: can a single low-cost orthogonal-strip spatial gate improve CENTER peak selection and cross-sequence center localization when the protected M1 backbone, RGB/TIR search fusion, original CENTER weights, size/offset branches and BN statistics are frozen? This is a **task-adapted Strip-inspired module**, not a faithful reproduction of Strip R-CNN's complete backbone or ROI head. Official Strip-R-CNN repository was audited at commit `c2d3cf4f9c561f4dea6d6779bcc7aaf3c5ae1b78`. The inserted module preserves its 5x5 depthwise, orthogonal strip depthwise, 1x1 projection and multiplicative gate core, but changes strip width from 19 to 7 for the 16x16 tracking map and adds a zero-initialized outer gate. It sits only after CENTER score branch `conv1_ctr` at `[B,256,16,16]`; the size and offset branches are untouched. It has 76,545 trainable parameters and about 19.3M MACs per 16x16 feature map. New experiment ID and outputs are independent of protected M1 and all LCGA runs.
