The full-244 DCVR evaluation is complete and its reported metrics are below the registered M1 point estimates on all five quality metrics. However, direct comparability is unresolved because the persisted M1 prediction directory does not reproduce its protected full-pipeline AUC, and the software environments differ. The result is classified as `FULL244-COMPLETE / RAW-POINT-DROP / COMPARABILITY-UNRESOLVED / PERFORMANCE-GAIN-NOT-SUPPORTED`. DCVR remains an information-flow diagnostic asset, but should not be promoted as the main method without resolving provenance and the confirmed train/test foreground-mask mismatch.
